# Employee Handbook (Demo)

## Time off
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year. Vacation requests should be submitted in the HR portal at least five working days before the requested time off. Managers review requests within two working days. Unused vacation does not roll over into the next year unless local law requires it.

## Expenses
Employees should submit business expense reports with itemized receipts within 30 days of purchase. Reports are reviewed by the manager and reimbursed in the next regular payment cycle after approval. Company cards should be used for travel booked through the approved travel portal.

## Flexible work
Teams may work remotely up to three days each week with manager agreement. Employees should keep their calendar current and be available during their team's agreed core hours, typically 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. local time.
