# Security Policy (Demo)

## Reporting an incident
Report suspected security incidents immediately to security@example.com or through the Security Incident form in the company help center. Include what happened, when it was noticed, and any affected systems. Do not investigate or delete potential evidence on your own. The security team acknowledges reports within one business hour and coordinates containment and recovery.

## Passwords and access
Use the company password manager to create a unique password for every work account. Multi-factor authentication is required for company services. Request access through the approved access request form; managers must approve access to sensitive systems. Remove access promptly when a role changes or employment ends.

## Handling company data
Store company documents only in approved company-managed services. Share files with the smallest appropriate audience and check link permissions before sending. Do not place confidential company data in public tools or personal accounts.
